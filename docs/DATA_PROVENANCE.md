## How the 0–100 index uses each layer

Slope and roughness describe coarse terrain gradients and elevation variability. Thermal is relative rendered THEMIS brightness, not temperature. Ice is a relative neutron-suppression proxy, not an ice fraction. Science and base distance use approximate catalog points. MEDA (Jezero) and REMS (Gale) daily series are historical point context for charts and do not enter the score.

## Known limitations

Mapped water-ice evidence indicates presence or likelihood, not extractability or accessibility.

Absence of a mapped hazard does not mean the terrain is safe.

The 0–100 index is a conditional heuristic methodology for comparison, not a safety certification or a validated model.

MEDA and REMS series are complete daily files for sampled sols (every 36th MEDA sol, every 96th REMS sol), without rover-heating correction; wind is omitted. Odyssey uses latitude bands -5.5 to -3.5 and 17.5 to 19.5 degrees, normalized against the maximum flux in those bands. Zero-valued neutron bins are missing observations, not ice evidence. Landing-site coordinates come from the PDS Geosciences ODE landing-site table or peer-reviewed publications (see source_ref per feature); science-target positions are approximate. Global ice bands are generalized from publications and are not ice maps. MOLA cannot resolve EVA-scale obstacles. No operational safety or extractability claims are made.
